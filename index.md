# SmartKitchen

**SmartKitchen** (shown on Google sign-in screens as "Mailing Agent") is a back-office tool for restaurant operators selling on Uber Eats.

It helps operators recover money lost on delivery incidents:

- **Order reports:** retrieves the restaurant's Uber Eats order reports automatically.
- **Refund requests:** prepares and sends refund requests to Uber Eats support for orders that were stolen, cancelled or disputed, from the restaurant's own mailbox.
- **Follow-up:** reads Uber's replies, records which refunds were granted, and sends reminders when there is no answer.
- **Dashboard:** shows requests sent, refunds obtained and amounts recovered, per restaurant.

The app runs on the operator's computer and uses the operator's own Google account (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive) only to provide these features.
