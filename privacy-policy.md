# Privacy Policy – SmartKitchen

SmartKitchen is an operational monitoring tool designed to help restaurant operators manage their delivery operations through the Uber Eats API.

The system processes operational restaurant data such as store identifiers, order identifiers, and order status information to provide monitoring and operational insights.

SmartKitchen does not collect or store personal customer data such as names, addresses, or payment information.

All data accessed through the Uber Eats API is used only for operational monitoring for authorized restaurant operators.

SmartKitchen does not sell, share, or distribute any data to third parties.

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the SmartKitchen team.
